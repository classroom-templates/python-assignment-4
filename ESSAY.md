  # AI Use and Learning Reflection

Answer each question clearly, specifically, and in your own words.

Your responses must demonstrate **depth of thought and actual engagement with your work**. Short, vague, or trivial answers will not receive full credit.

Do not give answers such as:

> AI helped me solve problems.

or:

> I used a loop because it worked.

Instead, explain **what you did, why you did it, and what you learned or verified**.

Whenever possible, include a **specific example from your program, your testing, or your interaction with AI**.

Each question is worth **2 points**, for a total of **10 points**.

## 1. AI Use

What generative AI system did you use, and describe one specific way you used it to help design, debug, test, or improve this program.

Include a concrete example of a question you asked, a suggestion AI made, or a problem you worked through with AI.

## 2. Choice of Repetition Structure

Choose one loop from your program.

Explain why the type of loop you used was appropriate for that part of the problem and why another repetition structure would have been a poorer choice.

Refer to the actual behavior of your program in your explanation.

## 3. Loop State and Termination

Choose one loop from your program and explain:

- what program state changes during each iteration;
- what condition controls whether the loop continues; and
- why the loop eventually terminates.

Use specific variables or conditions from your own code.

## 4. Exception Handling

Describe one place where your program uses exception handling.

Explain:

- what specific operation is being attempted;
- what specific failure is being handled;
- why exception handling is appropriate in that situation; and
- why the `try` block is appropriately narrow.

Refer to the actual code or behavior in your program.

## 5. Testing and Verification

Describe one test you performed that was specifically designed to challenge your control-flow logic.

Explain:

- what input or situation you tested;
- what behavior you expected;
- what actually happened; and
- what the result demonstrated about your program.

Use a specific example rather than a general statement such as "I tested my program and it worked."
